This sandbox is NOT MuJoCo physics: the browser cannot run the contact solver. It moves the base with a kinematic unicycle: v = 0.9776 r (wL + wR)/2 and w = r (wR - wL)/B_eff with B_eff = 1.4314 m (geometric 0.34 m), both fitted to MuJoCo by 14_mobile_bases.py. Same controller and gains as the script, dt 0.01 s; 14_check_browser_copy.py shows it matches its Python copy to 4e-16. Its planner uses the same formulas as its world, so open loop is perfect until you move the disturbance slider. The dashed paths and the numbers above replay runs recorded from real MuJoCo.
