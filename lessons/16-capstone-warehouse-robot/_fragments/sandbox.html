This sandbox replays runs recorded from MuJoCo by 16_capstone_lesson.py; it does not simulate. Samples every 0.1 s while the arm works and every 0.2 s while the base drives, rounded to 1 cm and 0.01 rad; the picture fills the gaps by straight-line interpolation. The yellow line is the arm seen from above; the dot turns teal when both touch sensors read more than 0.5 N.
