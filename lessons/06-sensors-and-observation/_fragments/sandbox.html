Noise off: values are exactly what MuJoCo puts in sensordata.

Starts at 0.7 rad, at rest. As in MuJoCo right after mj_step, sensordata describes the start of the last step, so it is one step behind qpos. Moving the slider sets data.ctrl; motor_force (before the gear, equal to ctrl) changes on the next step, and the hinge feels 100 times it in N m. The pendulum and sensor maths were checked against MuJoCo 3.15: all 9 values agree to better than 1e-13 (largest gap 3.9e-14) (4 decimals shown). The noise switch is added by this page, with the std values stored in model.sensor_noise (0.01 rad, 0.05 rad/s, 0.2 m/s²); MuJoCo itself does not add noise.
